Retracts the EXPOSURES.md entry "CA01's licence grace ends about 2026-09-15", which rests on
a GracePeriodRemaining of 14396 minutes read at 2026-09-05T21:27Z, minutes after the install.

Same pattern as DC01, captured in evidence/31 the same session. A short OOB Grace figure read
immediately after install or restart is transient and is not the settled evaluation period.

What this proves
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- CA01 reports LicenseStatus 1 (Licensed), not 2 (OOB Grace).
- GracePeriodRemaining is 255815 minutes, which is 177.65 days, ending about 2027-03-04.
- RemainingWindowsReArmCount is 6, the untouched original. CA01 has never been rearmed,
  so no rearm can account for the difference from the 14396-minute reading on 2026-09-05.
- The 14396-minute figure and the 255815-minute figure describe the same install, with no
  intervening rearm. The earlier figure was a post-install transient.

Consequence for the lab's calendar. Neither Windows Server guest expires in September 2026.
DC01 runs to about 2027-03-02 (evidence/31) with 5 rearms left. CA01 runs to about
2027-03-04 with 6 rearms left.
