--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What this proves
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The issuing CA certificate is published in the enterprise NTAuth store. Its hash,
  286c3546968d71398de9c5276a4192039605454a, matches the issuing CA element read in
  evidence/32. "Signature test passed".
- The root certificate is published in the enterprise Trusted Root store. Its hash,
  578ea69b32fc2e21b190ac0a24dc2e947adde045, matches the root element read in evidence/32.
  "Signature matches Public Key" and "Root Certificate: Subject matches Issuer".
- Both stores hold exactly one certificate each. No stale or competing CA entry exists.

Recorded, not interpreted:
- "ERROR: missing key association property: CERT_KEY_IDENTIFIER_PROP_ID" appears under the
  NTAuth entry. This is a local key-handle property on the machine that holds the private
  key. No source in this lab maps it to a functional consequence. Do not cite a meaning.
- "Cannot find the certificate and private key for decryption" appears under the root entry.
  CA01 does not hold the root's private key by design; the root lives offline in container
  106.
