What this proves
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- jsmith's live token on CA01 carries DISTRICT\PKI-CBA-Pilot, SID
  S-1-5-21-2288391267-384259729-2991373820-1133, "Enabled group".
- jsmith's client enumerates 34 templates and Template[9] is the client-auth template.
  The template is VISIBLE to jsmith. Read and Enroll are both present for
  PKI-CBA-Pilot. Rights are not the blocker.
- DISTRICT\Domain Users holds Allow Enroll on Template[9], via the SDDL ACE
  (OA;;RPWPCR;0e10c968-78fb-11d2-90d4-00c04f79dc55;;DU). So do Domain Admins and
  Enterprise Admins. DISTRICT\tmp-cainstall holds Allow Full Control.
- The built-in ClientAuth template carries the identical Domain Users Enroll ACE.
- certreq -enroll, given the FRIENDLY name, reports "Template not found" from the
  Active Directory Enrollment Policy over ldap:, then fails with 0x80094801
  CERTSRV_E_NO_CERT_TYPE.
