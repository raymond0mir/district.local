## Finding

Removing a security principal from a privileged AD group does not revoke rights already carried
in a live logon's Kerberos ticket. The directory-side removal at evidence/25 was correct and
immediate. The console session that outlived it kept acting as Enterprise Admins for at least
22 minutes, long enough to create and fully control a new PKI trust object (the certificate
template), with no further approval step and no additional log entry distinguishing "acting on
stale privilege" from "acting on current privilege."

This generalizes past this exercise. Any group-membership revocation -- a PIM deactivation, a
manual admin removal, an incident response action pulling someone from a privileged group --
leaves already-open sessions unaffected until they end. A written policy that "access is removed
immediately" is true of the directory and false of the session. Forcing sign-out, or capping
Kerberos ticket lifetime and Conditional Access sign-in frequency, is what actually closes the
gap. Neither is configured in this tenant or domain. Candidate for EXPOSURES.md.
