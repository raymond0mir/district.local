The pipeline

Four independent checks cover the path from drive to iCloud

Files leave the drive over USB, pass through the Mac mini's internal disk, and upload to iCloud Photos. Photos removes a local original after it reaches iCloud, so the internal disk is a buffer. Each check must pass before a batch counts as done.

The tech

osxphotos and Photos.app import the files. My own scripts check their work.

The importer's own counts proved unreliable in the first week, so every number it reports is checked by a second tool.

Hardware and services

Seagate drive
Backup Plus Desk, 2 TB, USB, HFS+, from 2012. The USB bridge blocks SMART, so the drive's health is unknown. It is read in place and was never imaged: the Mac has one free port.
Mac mini
macOS 26, about 460 GiB of internal disk. Every original passes through this disk before upload. Free space limits the whole run.
iCloud Photos
The destination, through Photos.app with Optimize Mac Storage on. Photos removes a local original after it is in iCloud.
cloudphotod
The macOS upload service. Its log shows low-disk refusals and download events.
External SSD
Took 25 GB of Lightroom renders and an iMovie backup image to free local space. It is not on the photo path.

Imports a folder in 500-group chunks. Each chunk is checked against its own dry run, and the run is paced to the upload. A full-path skip list holds files that are refused or decided out.

The import, damaged-video, library-check, Live Photo and round-trip scripts each have a fixture suite with deliberate mutants. A broken check fails its own test before it runs on real data.

How a batch runs

One folder, cut into chunks that check themselves and wait for the upload

  1. Probe the videos. mov-atoms-batch.sh reads every clip in the folder, with four known control files. Clips with no media data go on a known-bad list. A second scan reads every Live Photo content ID.
  2. Map the folder. One dry run of the whole folder records how osxphotos groups files. Live Photo videos are held back by full path, to be attached after their stills.
  3. Write the expected results down. They are committed to git before anything runs.
  4. Import in chunks. Each chunk gets its own dry run, then the import, then a reconcile. Any error stops the run, and it resumes where it stopped.
  5. Pace to the upload. Before each chunk, the album's not-in-iCloud count must be at or below what the last chunk imported. Below 30 GiB free, the run switches to small serial steps.
  6. Attach the Live Photo videos. A second pass joins each held-back video to its still. The inventories before and after must show every attach and no new asset.
  7. Drain. After the last chunk, the run waits until nothing in the album is still waiting to upload.
  8. Watch. caffeinate keeps the Mac awake. Claude watches the evidence files and reports progress during long runs.

The pacing exists because import runs at about 20 GiB/h and upload at about 3.95 GB/h. When the import outran the upload, the disk filled with originals Photos could not remove, and the upload stopped.

The four checks

A batch is done when four independent checks pass. The importer's own count is not one of them.

01
Count

Imported plus skipped must equal the chunk's own dry run, with 0 errors.

The first version compared the library's growth with the importer's claim. That comparison agrees with the importer by construction, and it cannot see a lost file.

Why it existsOn 2026-09-21 the old comparison reported a clean batch. That batch had lost 5,874 groups, and osxphotos had exited 0.

02
Upload

The album's count of assets not yet in iCloud must reach 0.

osxphotos reads the upload flag for each asset. The run waits for the drain, so "done" means uploaded.

03
Fingerprint

Every source file must match a live Photos asset by Photos' own fingerprint.

Each file lands in one class: LIVE, NOT-MEDIA, KNOWN-BAD, TRASH-ONLY or UNMATCHED. TRASH-ONLY and UNMATCHED must be 0. A Live Photo video stored inside its still's asset matches by content ID instead (LIVE-BY-ID). A reverse pass looks for album assets with no source file. For 2015/CRS: 10,844 media files LIVE, 0 unmatched, 0 strays.

Why it existsThis check found all three duplicate-skipping losses listed below. The importer reported 0 errors in each run.

04
Bytes

A seeded sample of evicted originals is downloaded back and compared with the drive by SHA-256.

The sample and its seed are committed before any download. 2015/CRS: 62 of 62 identical, across CR2, NEF, JPG, MOV and PNG. 2017/ip2017: 63 of 63 non-Live originals identical.

Why it existsPhotos marked steps "in iCloud" after about 12–17% of their bytes had left the Mac. The cause is not established, so the flag is not proof.

  • The expected results are written down before a check runs.
  • Every probe includes known control files. A control that misses stops the run.
  • A check is never relaxed after its result is seen.

What the checks caught

Fifteen problems. Four of them lose files without an error.

All four silent losses were caught, and every affected file was recovered. Newest first.

  • 2026-10-05Photos imported 2 Live Photo videos as separate clips. They were meant to join their stills. Found by the attach check, which expected 1,679 attaches and saw 1,677. The cause is not established, and the clips stay as they are.
  • 2026-10-03osxphotos reports a Live Photo attach as an error. A run stopped on 10 "failed" videos, and Photos hung after the restarts. All 10 were in the library as Live Photos. Live videos now go in a second pass, matched by content ID and proven by inventory.
  • 2026-10-02Photos refused the two locked files on the drive. Both are valid JPEGs with the Finder Locked flag. A scan of the whole drive found exactly 2 locked files, and Photos refused exactly those two. Both are held out by full path.
  • 2026-10-01Duplicate skipping matched 23 files to shared-album items.silent loss Shared-album items are not in my own library, so those files were missing. Found by a round-trip pilot and a trace of every fingerprint-less match. Fixed by a targeted import; all 1,235 Masters/ files are now LIVE.
  • 2026-10-01osxphotos misreported two imports. It called one file an error after Photos imported it, and it called another clean but left it out of the album. Found by a one-file fingerprint check and a per-UUID album check. A refused import is now checked before any resume.
  • 2026-09-30Duplicate skipping dropped 14 Live Photo pairs.silent loss Each still matched an older still-only asset, so the whole pair was skipped. Found by the library check. The pairs were re-imported as Live Photos, and 28 of 28 files are SHA-256-identical to the drive.
  • 2026-09-30Duplicate skipping dropped a different file.silent loss r1.jpg had the same name and size as another file, but different content. Found by the library check as UNMATCHED. Fixed by a targeted re-import without duplicate skipping. It happened again on 2026-10-02 with 44.jpeg, and osxphotos' source shows the cause: a filename-and-size fallback.
  • 2026-09-3036 recovered video files hold an index but no media data. They stop an import, and osxphotos' --stop-on-error restarted Photos instead of stopping. Found by a stopped run, then by the atom walk with control files. Every batch's videos are now probed first.
  • 2026-09-29Duplicate skipping does not work inside a burst. 87 byte-identical copies went in as extra burst frames. Found by a burst duplicate census. burst-map.py now drops those copies when the map is built.
  • 2026-09-29A new chunk raced Photos' startup. The first import call failed with "User canceled (-128)". The script now confirms Photos is ready before each chunk.
  • 2026-09-27Photos marked steps "in iCloud" after about 12–17% of their bytes had left the Mac. Found by logging the network byte counter each minute. Answered by the round trip.
  • 2026-09-24Upload deadlock. The disk filled with originals not yet uploaded, Photos set low-disk, and the upload stopped. Free space reached 1.49 GiB. About 80 GiB was freed on the Mac, and the import was paced to the upload.
  • 2026-09-21osxphotos exited 0 after 5,900 failed imports.silent loss The library count still matched the importer's claim. Found by comparison with the dry run's group count. Fixed by the chunked importer, and all 5,874 lost groups were imported.
  • 2026-09-19The iPhoto catalogs are incomplete. The 2014 catalog lists 4,970 of its 14,107 masters, and macOS 26 cannot open these libraries. The Masters/ folders are imported directly. A library import would have missed about 9,000 files.
  • 2026-09-19The working figure was 945 GB. The drive held 1.74 TB, and iCloud had 832 items from those years: the migration had not started. Every figure is now captured again before it is used.
