district.local · before the first exercise

Home Away From Home

To be displaced, to recreate feeling from memory, in my humble opinion, is akin to the alchemist's pursuit to make iron into gold.

Raymond Miranda  ·  Los Angeles

I was AFK, the hum of multiple workstations in progress of staging, loud fan exhaust from a to be configured network device, the many OOBE screens, and most attentive, the talking Mac computers after a reset. I never thought to myself, yeah ima miss that.

Entra, Azure, Active Directory, the big complicated rolodex (old person talk for something we used to keep on our desk with all our contacts on flashcards) in the sky now, before in the closet, on one of those screaming blades, anyone who dared to be in its on prem presence lest be warned, none with bad luck enter it's holding, now in a much bigger closet, somewhere in between the sea cables and my browser window, i long for Microsoft UI choices, the familiar red green blue yellow square, you can call yourself whatever you want, you will always be something i clicked around in ritual.

One click here, one click there, bad click oh no, deleted a whole group and over 100 people no longer have access to their work required resource. I cried out loud, WHOOPSIE, in jest but also as one does when one mistakes, the tickets started to come in, that was the first sign, the realization that was the other, the hurried Teams messages to the Senior System Admin, well those messages i hope he keeps in secret, i must have sounded desperate and willingly to pay for his lunch for the next week, he was able to restore the group's SSO access, the user list was another issue.

I thought quickly and exported a list of associates who had access and ran it across the tickets coming in, cross compared to the access list within the program and ended up with nearly 15 open licenses from people who either didn't need access anymore, or their use case expired. I won't get into the cost of these licenses, but it was a pretty penny per seat.

Now with the Entra group's recreated with the sysadmin's help, I assigned the up to date list and quickly resolved tickets before it could bubble up to a C suite level issue, naturally it did but by that time, it was fixed with hardly any downtime.

DELETE MEANS DELETE :) the sysadmin's words of wisdom

A slip up i would not forget. I cautiously approached the Entra URL each time, ensuring, clicks done in ritual, weren't in muscle memory and intention with stated goal became second nature.

I would be asked to clear up more licenses, each time having to leverage Entra's record against excel exports of badly formatted emails, misshaped names, and sometimes rarely, the export came out good and there's no Entra entry. Discovery meant hours in front of lists, comparing and contrasting, once Copilot came into the equation, it became a true force multiplier in the Microsoft Office ecosystem. List sorting, differences, repeats, and misspellings became extremely fast to go through.

PowerShell to most help desk is cmd's scary older brother with more access, my spiral into would be one of necessity not so much escalation. Most would chalk up PowerShell to scripts or other wizardly scrolls the system admin's would project down to us lowly help desk apprentices, we would copy, paste the alien language into the scary admin computer mind screen, some times it would return red text, FEAR, most times it would be long paragraphs of familiar symbols, maybe you can even read the very legible reason why PowerShell shot back your cmd.

Either way, most of my colleagues weren't using it daily, nor would dare to open it. I wasn't so much fearless but emboldened...with the flame of knowledge.

I commanded Copilot to craft PowerShell scripts, i would then use with the sorted list and like magic, Active/ Not Active would return, seemingly hours of work done by hand delegated in sections to a team of 10, done in a 30 min jam session. I say jam, cause i probably spent 20 mins finding the right song to coder LARP my PowerShell session with.

Like a true Wizard of the people, i passed this sacred knowledge to my coworkers.

That was nearly a year ago. The state of AI has advanced, my own interpretations of the hype grew leery, not so much because of the doom and gloom or the unrealized promises, but the lack of usage by the common everyday menfolk, who may call upon an AI LLM Frontier model to mindlessly answer an otherwise not important email to their favorite cohort.

I realize, I'm painting a extreme on one end, but day to day, our enduring relationship with end users, tells me it could peak amazingly into the Power user embracing AI and letting it have a go at the dev team's repo, read/write by default baby! or soon, i'll be troubleshooting my mom's AI TV because it won't stop recommending Home Shopping Network.

I focus my own studies on something clearer in mind, the 4 colored square of micro softness, the azure colored blue borders how it shines so Entrally.

With time and a fresh memory in mind, i set my goal

Remake Active Directory From Memory

I used it daily, i longed for it in it's absence, but it wasn't the Senior System Admin's vision of AD, it was my twisted spirally ritual be damned clicked memory of the shape, the rolodex on my desk in my dream of the 90's office man business day.

If a carpenter hammers nails daily, he might get a nice groove in his trall, he might even begin to figure, i can easily yank nails out with the back of this hammer, the wind up of the carpenters arm begins to form a click with each day of work. The carpenter is good with the hammer, he fix issues with the hammer, but the novice carpenter sees nails everywhere, the wisen hammer slinger, well they just get to swinging, nail or not, something will get hammered in. As the novice carpenter admires the many holes the wisen carpenter has made, finding studs by feeling, the novice becomes intermediate when he forms the connection —

Not everything is a nail

and

I'm not a hammer

So, the intermediate studies the blueprints, day after day, the expansion of the inter framework begins to appear in the nail eyed hammer bammer, he no longer sees nails everywhere, the electricians, who he just assumed showed up got paid and drank mtn dew, had methods to their madness, the plumbers pipes, who the hammer head bursted more than few times, didn't need a stern ear to be heard through the walls, he knew where the pipes stood.

The middle man, the in betweener, not adept enough to wisely steer studs, not brainy enough to blue a print, he was observant and possessed enough to dream, what if i built my own damn house? Wait thats too ambitious, the best the malcom in middle could imagine would be the structure of a house, if he observed it in detail, committed it to his memory, with the help of the world's leading do everything magic tech, surely he can prove to the wisen carpenter that his placement at the table is worth keeping.

With the longing in mind, i got to work.
